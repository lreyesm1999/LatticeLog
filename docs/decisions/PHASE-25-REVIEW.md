# PHASE-25-REVIEW — Commercial product, licensing and SaaS

- Status: **N/A** (owner decision, 2026-11-18)
- Decision: LatticeLog is a **free and open project under the MIT license**. There will be no paid editions, SaaS or
  billing. See ADR-0012 in `docs/decisions/ADR-0002-to-0008.md` and `docs/product/commercial-decisions.md`.
- What still applies from this phase: portability (T04, done: documented format and independent reader) and leaving
  without lock-in (T18, done: lossless export and verifiable erasure).
