# Code of Conduct

LatticeLog follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- No publishing of other people's private information.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that don't follow it, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/lreyesm1999). Reports are handled confidentially.
